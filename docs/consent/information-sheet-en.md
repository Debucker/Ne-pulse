# NE-PULSE: information for students and parents

**Project.** NE-PULSE is a school research project: can ordinary phones and low-cost sensors notice earthquakes together? Supervisor at school: ______________________ (name, contact).

**Taking part is voluntary.** Students can refuse or leave at any time, with no effect on grades.

## What the student does
Opens ne-pulse.com on their phone, enters class code ______ and leaves the phone lying on a table (ideally on a charger, for example overnight). While the page is open, the phone sends its motion-sensor readings.

## What is collected
- Motion-sensor (accelerometer) readings, 50–60 per second, **only while the NE-PULSE page is open**.
- With a class code, these readings are **recorded in full** for the class's research project.
- The 1 × 1 km square the phone lies in (computed on the phone; the exact position is never sent).
- A random device number and the phone type (iPhone/Android).

## What is NOT collected
Name, phone number, email, photos, microphone, camera, contacts, exact location. The IP address is not stored.

## Who sees the data, and for how long
- Class recordings are seen only by the project supervisor and the student researchers on this project, under pseudonyms (no device number).
- The public map shows only 10 km squares with at least three phones.
- Recordings are kept for up to ____ days (365 by default), then deleted.
- Results may appear in a research paper only in aggregated, anonymous form.

## How to leave
On the "Your phone" page, "Delete my data" removes the device number and every recording from that phone. For Telegram alerts, /stop in the bot deletes the subscription.

## Safety
NE-PULSE is an experiment, **not an official warning service**. Alerts may be late, wrong or missing. Always follow the Ministry of Emergency Situations of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
